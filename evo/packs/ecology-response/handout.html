Printable Focus

Question

What ecological relationship or environmental change are you testing?

Prediction

What do you think will happen, and why?

Evidence

What output, pattern, or species information supports your explanation?

Record Your Work

1. Define the change

Describe the factor you changed and the ecological system you are watching.

Write what you changed, what you kept fixed, and why that matters.

2. Compare runs

Record at least two runs or scenarios and note the most important differences.

Look for stable patterns, not just one surprising result.

3. Connect to organisms

Use EcoSpecies or another cited source to compare the model with a real organism or habitat.

What parts of the model seem helpful, and what parts are still oversimplified?

Revision Prompt

What changed your thinking?

Describe one idea you revised after comparing runs or reading a species account.

What evidence do you still need?

List one observation or source that would help you test your explanation more carefully.
