Study Pack

Ecology and environmental response.

This first ecology pack ties together ecological feedback, climate-driven change, and organism-level evidence so students compare model behavior with real systems and cited species information.

EcoBalance guide Climate Range Shifter guide Student handout High-school pathway
Primary fit HS-LS2-1, HS-LS2-2, HS-LS2-6, HS-LS4-5, and HS-ESS3-5.
Core tools EcoBalance, Climate Range Shifter, and EcoSpecies for evidence-grounded follow-up.

Pack Sequence

1. Frame the system

Use the Notebook or a short launch prompt to define the ecological relationship or environmental change under study.

2. Compare modeled response

Use EcoBalance or Climate Range Shifter for the core investigation.

3. Connect to organisms and sources

Use EcoSpecies and bibliography work to connect the model to organisms, habitats, and cited evidence.

Expected Student Work

Evidence products

  • A prediction about ecological response under changed conditions.
  • A comparison of runs tied to explicit observed outcomes.
  • A short explanation that connects model behavior to an organism or system claim.

Didactopus-style prompts

  • What in the model output supports your interpretation?
  • What real-world evidence or species account helps test that interpretation?
  • What result would make you revise your current ecological explanation?
