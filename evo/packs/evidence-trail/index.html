Study Pack

Evidence trail.

This self-learner pack moves from a focused question to interactive investigation, species evidence, bibliography growth, and reflective revision.

Notebook EcoSpecies Study log Literature Explorer
Primary fit Independent study that combines conceptual grounding, source work, and evidence-based revision.
Core tools Notebook, Scientific Virtues, one guide-led app, EcoSpecies, and Literature Explorer.

Pack Sequence

1. Frame a question

Start with the Notebook and Scientific Virtues to define the question and study posture.

2. Investigate actively

Choose one guide-led app such as Allele Tracker or EcoBalance and compare runs carefully.

3. Build the evidence trail

Use EcoSpecies and Literature Explorer to grow the bibliography and test your explanation against sources.

Expected Study Work

Working record

  • A written prediction before each run set.
  • A comparison of observed outputs and source-backed claims.
  • A revision note describing what changed and why.

Didactopus pattern

  • What did you observe?
  • What interpretation currently fits best?
  • What evidence would make you revise again?
