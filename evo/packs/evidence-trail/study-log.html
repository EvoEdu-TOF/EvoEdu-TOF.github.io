Working Structure

Question

What are you trying to understand or test?

Observation

What did the model, species account, or source actually show?

Interpretation

What explanation currently fits best, and what evidence supports it?

Revision

What changed your thinking, and what would you want to verify next?

Source Check

Which source is strongest?

List the source you trust most so far and why.

What remains uncertain?

Note one gap, conflict, or limitation that keeps the conclusion open.

Next evidence move

Write the next source, species record, or run comparison you plan to check.
