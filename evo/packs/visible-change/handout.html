Printable Focus

What changed?

Write down one clear change you noticed during the activity.

What stayed the same?

Note one thing that remained similar even when the run or system changed.

What is your evidence?

Record the observation, pattern, or organism detail that supports your explanation.

Record Your Work

1. Watch closely

Describe what you noticed first without trying to explain everything at once.

2. Compare two cases

Write how two runs, settings, or examples were alike and different.

3. Connect to a real organism

Use EcoSpecies to connect one model pattern to a real organism or habitat example.
