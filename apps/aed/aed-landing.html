Welcome to Avida-ED

Avida-ED is a powerful and award-winning digital evolution platform that brings the process of evolution directly to your classroom, developed and maintained by a team at Michigan State University. The Avida-ED applications let you explore how digital organisms mutate, compete, and adapt in dynamic environments. Whether you're an educator or an enthusiastic student, you'll find these tools both enlightening and engaging.

We offer two versions of Avida-ED here:

  • Avida-ED 3: This legacy web app is used for curriculum materials that have not yet been updated. It is ideal for educators who have existing lesson plans based on Avida-ED 3.
  • Avida-ED 4: Our newest version designed for new users and for those interested in developing ecological hypotheses. Avida-ED 4 offers enhanced features and an updated interface for a more modern teaching experience.

If you are interested in Avida-ED 2, the native app for MacOS, we unfortunately do not have that available here.

Avida-ED is a product of Michigan State University, and appears here under the terms of its open-source license.
