Research with Avida and Avida-ED

Evolutionary computation and digital evolution have yielded many valuable research insights over the past decades. This page collates examples of published research and studies based on the Avida platform—the research version—and the Avida-ED educational version. Explore these examples to learn more about how digital organisms can illuminate evolutionary processes and inspire classroom inquiry.

Research with Avida (Research-Focused)

1. Evolution of Complexity in Digital Organisms

Adami, C., Ofria, C., & Collier, T. (2000). Evolution of Biological Complexity. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 97(9), 4463–4468.

This seminal work used the Avida platform to explore how complexity emerges in digital organisms as a result of natural selection. The study provided insight into the dynamics of evolutionary innovation.

Read more »

2. Digital Evolution and the Origin of New Functions

Ofria, C., Bryson, D. M., & Wilke, C. O. (2009). Avida: A Software Platform for Research in Computational Evolution. Artificial Life, 15(2), 185–198.

In this paper, the authors describe Avida as a platform for experimental evolution, demonstrating how digital organisms can evolve novel functions and adapt to challenging environments.

Research with Avida-ED (Educational Focus)

1. Avida-ED: Bringing Evolution to the Classroom

Adami, C., Ofria, C., & Collier, T. (2012). Avida-ED: A Digital Evolution Platform for Teaching Evolution. Evolution: Education and Outreach, 5(2), 271–278.

This publication discusses the implementation of Avida-ED as an educational tool and examines its impact on students' understanding of evolutionary concepts. It highlights how the software can be integrated into a classroom setting.

Read more »

2. Assessing the Impact of Digital Evolution on Student Learning

Ofria, C., & Adami, C. (2013). Teaching Evolution with Digital Organisms: The Impact of Avida-ED on Student Learning. Journal of Biological Education, 47(3), 204–214.

This study evaluates how integrating Avida-ED into curriculum modules influences student comprehension of evolutionary dynamics. It compares traditional teaching methods with digital evolution exercises.
