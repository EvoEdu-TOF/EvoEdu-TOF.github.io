Avida-ED Exercises

Avida-ED is a digital evolution platform that brings evolution into the classroom. At evo-edu.org, our Avida-ED exercises are designed to demonstrate fundamental evolutionary concepts in a dynamic, interactive way.

Through these exercises, students explore:

  • The emergence of heritable variation via mutation
  • The role of selection in driving adaptation
  • How digital organisms compete and evolve in changing environments
  • The influence of environmental pressures on evolutionary outcomes

Each exercise includes step-by-step instructions, data visualization tools, and discussion questions that encourage students to connect digital evolution with real-world biological processes.
