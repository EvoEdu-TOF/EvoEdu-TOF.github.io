Avida-ED and K–12 Science Standards

Our Avida-ED exercises are designed to align with key K–12 science standards, especially the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS). The digital evolution experiments facilitate inquiry into core evolutionary processes, making abstract concepts concrete through interactive simulation.

Middle School (MS) Standards

MS-LS4-4: Students construct explanations based on evidence to describe how genetic variations increase the probability of survival and reproduction.
  • Application: Avida-ED exercises show how digital organisms mutate, compete, and adapt—illustrating heritable variation and natural selection.
  • Inquiry: Students adjust mutation and selection parameters to predict and observe changes in digital populations.

High School (HS) Standards

HS-LS4-2: Students explain how evolution results from variation, competition for limited resources, and differential survival and reproduction.
  • Application: Avida-ED provides a platform for observing the interplay of mutation, selection, and genetic drift in real time.
  • Inquiry: Through guided experiments, students explore how environmental pressures alter evolutionary outcomes and generate data to compare with theoretical models.

In addition to addressing specific performance expectations, Avida-ED exercises foster crosscutting concepts such as:

  • Cause and Effect: Manipulating environmental variables leads to observable changes in population dynamics.
  • Systems Thinking: Understanding how individual behaviors and interactions contribute to larger evolutionary patterns.
  • Data Analysis: Students collect and interpret quantitative data, developing mathematical representations of biological processes.

These exercises not only meet curricular requirements but also encourage critical thinking and scientific inquiry.
