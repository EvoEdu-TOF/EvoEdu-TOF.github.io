Avida-ED: Digital Evolution Platform

What it is: Avida-ED is not a simulation of evolution—it is a real instance of evolution by natural selection in a digital medium. Digital organisms replicate, mutate, and compete for CPU cycles; those that perform computational tasks gain a replication advantage, fulfilling all conditions for natural selection.

Why This Matters

Avida-ED demonstrates that natural selection is a logical consequence of variation, heredity, and differential reproduction—regardless of whether the replicators are made of DNA or code. As explained in the Notebook, this refutes the claim that evolution is “unobservable”: here, it unfolds in real time. The platform also illustrates the difference between adaptive change (task-performing lineages) and non-adaptive change (neutral mutations), reinforcing key conceptual distinctions.
