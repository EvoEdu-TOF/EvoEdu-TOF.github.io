Launch notes

Use the application alongside the learner guide and teacher guide. The environment is most useful when learners enter with a clear question, collect observations from runs, and compare outcomes across repeated trials.

What to do first

For learners

  • Start with a prediction about mutation, selection pressure, or historical contingency.
  • Run more than one trial so chance remains visible.
  • Save screenshots or logs as evidence for your explanation.

For instructors

  • Use the teacher guide to frame the experiment before launch.
  • Use the standards page to connect the activity to evidence and systems thinking.
  • Use the about page to keep provenance and model limits explicit.
