Digital Evolution Lab standards and alignment

The strongest fit is with evolution, inheritance, evidence, and systems thinking: learners make predictions, inspect changing populations, and justify explanations from observed outcomes.

Alignment Notes

Disciplinary core ideas

  • LS4: biological evolution, common mechanisms, and evidence from change over time
  • LS3: inheritance and variation as they shape evolutionary outcomes

Science practices and crosscutting concepts

  • Analyzing and interpreting data from repeated runs
  • Constructing explanations from observed evidence
  • Cause and effect, systems, and stability/change

Scientific Virtues in Use

Digital Evolution Lab is most useful when learners compare repeated runs, state model limits honestly, and revise claims when a favored result does not generalize. That makes it a good companion to the broader Scientific Virtues materials.
