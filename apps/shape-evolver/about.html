Shape Evolver about and provenance

Shape Evolver is the evo-edu public route for biomorph-style artificial selection. The route now launches the newer Biomorph implementation as its current interactive engine.

Lineage

Current Limits

  • The runtime is a pedagogical browser model, not a biological-development simulator.
  • The displayed forms are generated phenotypes controlled by simplified integer genes.
