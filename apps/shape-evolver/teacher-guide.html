Shape Evolver teacher notes and classroom moves

Use Shape Evolver to make cumulative selection visible, but keep the distinction clear between this artificial-selection model and biological evolution in populations.

Open application Learner guide Standards

Teacher focus

What this tool is best for

  • Showing how small inherited changes can accumulate over repeated selection.
  • Separating random variation from nonrandom choice among variants.
  • Discussing model limits rather than pretending the app is a full biological simulation.

What to avoid

  • Do not present the chooser as equivalent to natural selection in an ecosystem.
  • Do not imply the nine-number gene model is realistic molecular genetics.
  • Do not let learners leave thinking that one dramatic final form is the point rather than the cumulative history of selection.

Suggested lesson flow

Short lesson

  1. Orient learners to parent, offspring grid, mutation legend, and selection history.
  2. Give a visible goal, such as taller, wider, or more branching.
  3. Run 10 to 15 generations and export a final image.
  4. Ask what was inherited, what was selected, and what the model leaves out.

Longer lesson

  1. Have groups pursue different selection goals from the same starting form.
  2. Compare manual choice with one automated evaluator.
  3. Use saved configurations and histories as evidence in short written explanations.
  4. End with a model-boundary discussion: what would have to change to study drift, population frequencies, or ecological selection?

Assessment and evidence

Useful artifacts

  • Exported image of the final form
  • Saved configuration JSON
  • Selection-history entries
  • Written explanation of two meaningful changes

Strong student response

A strong explanation distinguishes inherited instructions from visible phenotype, identifies the role of repeated selection, and names at least one important model limitation.

Relationship to Biomorphs

The current Shape Evolver runtime is the newer Biomorphs implementation. This teacher guide keeps the public evo-edu route coherent while using that stronger responsive application underneath.
