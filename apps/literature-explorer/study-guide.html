Teaching Focus

Teacher notes

  • Start from a narrow question or seed reading instead of a broad topic word.
  • Have learners justify why each added source belongs in the bibliography trail.
  • Use source comparison to discuss evidence quality, relevance, and scope.

Learner tasks

  • Begin with one seed source and identify what it suggests looking at next.
  • Record why each added citation matters to the question.
  • Summarize how the bibliography trail changed the original understanding.
