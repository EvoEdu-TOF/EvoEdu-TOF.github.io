Curriculum Alignment

How Literature Explorer fits NGSS-oriented work

Literature Explorer supports science practices more than a single disciplinary core idea: it helps learners ask questions, evaluate sources, and organize evidence around scientific claims.

Strongest alignment zones

Science and engineering practices

  • Asking questions and refining them through evidence gathering
  • Obtaining, evaluating, and communicating information
  • Using evidence from multiple sources to support explanations

Crosscutting support

  • Cause and effect through literature comparison
  • Systems and system models through linked topics and citation networks
  • Patterns in how evidence accumulates across related studies

Alignment note

Literature Explorer is best used as a support layer for other evo-edu.org platforms, especially EcoSpecies, Notebook work, and teacher-built reading paths.
