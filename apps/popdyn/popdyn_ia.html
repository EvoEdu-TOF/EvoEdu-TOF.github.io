Matrix Population Dynamics Setup

This app demonstrates how to:

  • Set up age or stage classes for a population.
  • Input transition probabilities and fecundity rates.
  • Analyze how populations grow, stabilize, or decline over time.
  • Create “what‐if” scenarios for species under various conditions or threats.

Tips:
- “Survival to Next Stage” means if you’re in Stage i, probability of moving to Stage i+1.
- “Recurrence” often means remaining in the same stage for one more time step.
- “Fecundity” refers to how many new individuals are produced by a stage (they typically go into the first stage).
- You can experiment with any transition structure you like.
