Evidence And Cause Workbench

The app supports scientific practices that cut across evolution, genetics, and ecology units.

Standards-Facing Evidence

  • Students distinguish empirical observations from explanations.
  • Students use models as baselines and identify model assumptions.
  • Students evaluate whether evidence supports a causal claim.
  • Students identify stochastic mechanisms and multi-cause alternatives.
  • Students distinguish empirical trend claims from progress, purpose, or value claims.
  • Students explain why a useful simple model may not explain every exception in a real dataset.
  • Students explain why simplified classroom conditions may differ from real-world analysis.
